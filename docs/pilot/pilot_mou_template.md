## Purpose

This MOU governs a non-commercial, time-limited pilot evaluation of the QASH offline incident-log commitment demonstrator. The evaluation is purely technical and exploratory; it creates no commercial obligations for either party.

---

## Scope of Evaluation

The partner will:

1. Install the QASH pilot baseline software on a local workstation.
2. Run the demonstration using synthetic incident data (not real incidents).
3. Evaluate the offline receipt issuance, public commitment export, deterministic replay, and selective disclosure capabilities.
4. Provide written feedback within [NUMBER] weeks.

The evaluation does not include:

- Production deployment or live incident ingestion.
- Payment, custody, financial settlement, or regulatory compliance activities.
- Any commitment to procure or deploy the software.

---

## Data Handling

- No real incident data, personally identifiable information, or sensitive operational data shall be used during the evaluation.
- The partner shall treat `disclosure.bin` outputs as confidential and not share them outside the evaluation team.
- Public artifacts (`public_commitments.bin`, `replay.json`) may be shared freely.

## Intellectual Property

- Neither party acquires any IP rights of the other under this MOU.
