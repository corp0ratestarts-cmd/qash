## Technical effect observed

1. Per-epoch cost scales roughly linearly with validator count: ~0.28 µs per
   validator at 1024 validators. This is dominated by the hash cascade in
   `encode_full_state_into` and SHA3-256 state-root computation, not by the
   Lyapunov fixed-point arithmetic.

2. Worst-case epoch transition (1024 validators, max divergence): 282 µs.
   The 450 ms spec budget provides approximately 1590× safety margin.

3. Serialization cost is ~8 µs encode / 7.4 µs decode at 1024 validators —
   below 2% of the 450 ms budget.

4. Replay scales linearly: 100 epochs × 4 validators = 1.54 ms total.
   Full genesis-to-current replay of 10,000 epochs with 4 validators would
   cost ~154 ms; with 1024 validators, ~2.83 s.

## Limitations

- Measurements on a shared cloud host; variance ~5%. Re-run on dedicated
  hardware for publication-grade numbers.
- Stack depth not measured here. EpochState is ~420 KB on stack (large
  fixed arrays). Tests pass with default Rust stack (8 MB) but production
  deployment should verify thread stack sizes for replay tasks.
- `advance_epoch` is called on a single thread; no concurrency modeling.
